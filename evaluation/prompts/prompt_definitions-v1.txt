Label definitions:
activate_my_card: Activating a newly received card so it can be used.
age_limit: Minimum age, age restrictions, or accounts for children.
apple_pay_or_google_pay: Using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a watch to top up.
atm_support: Which ATMs or cash machines are supported or where to find one.
automatic_top_up: Setting up, limits of, or problems with automatic balance replenishment.
balance_not_updated_after_bank_transfer: An incoming bank transfer was made but the account balance has not updated.
balance_not_updated_after_cheque_or_cash_deposit: A cash or cheque deposit has not appeared in the balance.
beneficiary_not_allowed: Transfers to a particular beneficiary or type of recipient are not allowed, including restricted cryptocurrency destinations.
cancel_transfer: Cancelling, reversing, or stopping a bank transfer, including one sent to the wrong account.
card_about_to_expire: Expiring or expired cards and their replacement or renewal.
card_acceptance: Where a card is accepted for purchases, including merchants or countries.
card_arrival: A card already ordered has not arrived; tracking or investigating its delivery status.
card_delivery_estimate: Expected card delivery duration, delivery date, or delivery options in general.
card_linking: Linking an existing card to the app/account, or reactivating a previously lost card that was found.
card_not_working: The physical card generally does not work or appears broken, without a more specific failure type.
card_payment_fee_charged: An extra fee for a purchase or payment made with the card.
card_payment_not_recognised: An unfamiliar card payment or merchant transaction the customer does not recognize.
card_payment_wrong_exchange_rate: An incorrect exchange rate on a card purchase.
card_swallowed: An ATM retained or swallowed the physical card.
cash_withdrawal_charge: A fee charged for withdrawing cash at an ATM.
cash_withdrawal_not_recognised: A recorded cash withdrawal the customer did not make or does not recognize.
change_pin: Changing the card PIN, including where and how to do so.
compromised_card: Suspected card details theft, cloning, or someone else using the card; securing or blocking the compromised card.
contactless_not_working: Contactless or tap-to-pay functionality is not working or needs enabling.
country_support: Countries of residence where customers can open accounts or access the service.
declined_card_payment: A purchase/payment was rejected or declined when attempted.
declined_cash_withdrawal: An attempted ATM withdrawal was declined or refused.
declined_transfer: A bank transfer was explicitly declined or rejected.
direct_debit_payment_not_recognised: An unfamiliar direct debit, or disputing/cancelling an unauthorized direct debit payment.
disposable_card_limits: Limits on the number or use of disposable virtual cards.
edit_personal_details: Editing account profile details such as name, address, or contact information.
exchange_charge: Fees for currency exchange itself, rather than an ATM or card transaction fee.
exchange_rate: Which exchange rate applies or how rates are calculated, without reporting a wrong transaction rate.
exchange_via_app: How to exchange currencies or convert money using the app.
extra_charge_on_statement: An extra small verification/authorization charge, commonly one pound, or an otherwise unspecified extra statement charge.
failed_transfer: A bank transfer failed or could not be completed without a more specific pending/declined/beneficiary condition.
fiat_currency_support: Fiat currencies that can be held, exchanged, or used in the account.
get_disposable_virtual_card: Obtaining or understanding a disposable, temporary, or single-use virtual card.
get_physical_card: Finding or retrieving the existing card PIN, including a PIN not received or forgotten. This dataset label means PIN retrieval despite its name.
getting_spare_card: Getting an additional/spare physical card on an existing account.
getting_virtual_card: Obtaining or accessing a regular virtual card, rather than a disposable card.
lost_or_stolen_card: A physical card is lost or stolen and needs locating, blocking, or replacing.
lost_or_stolen_phone: A phone is lost or stolen, including accessing the account without it.
order_physical_card: Ordering or obtaining a physical card, its availability or delivery locations. Do not use get_physical_card for this.
passcode_forgotten: Forgotten app login passcode/password or resetting account access credentials, rather than the card PIN.
pending_card_payment: A card purchase remains pending or uncompleted.
pending_cash_withdrawal: An ATM cash withdrawal is marked pending, even if cash was received.
pending_top_up: A top-up remains pending, incomplete, or processing.
pending_transfer: A transfer is explicitly marked pending or processing.
pin_blocked: The card PIN is blocked after too many incorrect attempts; unblocking it.
receiving_money: Receiving salary or money from another person, including how others can pay into the account.
refund_not_showing_up: An expected or requested refund has not arrived or is missing from the account.
request_refund: Requesting a refund for a purchase or cancelling a purchase to obtain a refund.
reverted_card_payment: A card payment was reverted, cancelled, or returned automatically; asking why money returned.
supported_cards_and_currencies: Cards or currencies accepted for adding money by card top-up.
terminate_account: Closing, deleting, or terminating the account.
top_up_by_bank_transfer_charge: Fees for receiving money or funding the account through bank transfer/SEPA.
top_up_by_card_charge: Fees for funding the account by card, including cards from other countries.
top_up_by_cash_or_cheque: Whether or how cash or cheques can be used to fund the account.
top_up_failed: A card top-up failed, was declined, or could not be completed.
top_up_limits: Amount limits or maximum amounts for manual top-ups.
top_up_reverted: A completed top-up was reversed or returned to its source.
topping_up_by_card: How card top-ups work, or a charged card top-up not yet reflected in the wallet, without an explicit pending/failed/reverted status.
transaction_charged_twice: The same purchase/payment was charged twice or duplicated.
transfer_fee_charged: Fees charged for sending/transferring money out of the account.
transfer_into_account: How to fund the account by transfer from another bank; transfer instructions or details.
transfer_not_received_by_recipient: An outgoing transfer was sent but the other recipient has not received it.
transfer_timing: General bank transfer processing/arrival times, including international transfers, without a specific pending state or missing completed transfer.
unable_to_verify_identity: Identity verification was attempted but failed, was rejected, or the app cannot recognize the documents/person.

verify_source_of_funds: Establishing or verifying the origin/source of money or funds.

visa_or_mastercard: Whether the card is Visa or Mastercard, or choosing between them.

wrong_amount_of_cash_received: The customer attempted an ATM withdrawal but received too little, no cash, or the wrong amount despite being charged.
wrong_exchange_rate_for_cash_withdrawal: An incorrect exchange rate on an ATM cash withdrawal.
